This page is served over plain HTTP, so the browser limits it: no offline play, the screen is not kept awake, and the browser may clear the game files when space runs low. Over HTTPS, or on localhost, none of this applies.

reG2

Play GTA2 in your browser with your own copy of the game.

How it works

  1. Give this page your copy of GTA2: the game folder, the free 2004 installer, the CD or a zip of it.
  2. The page unpacks it right here, in your browser, and keeps it in this site's storage on this device.
  3. Press Play. Next time the game is already here, even offline.

This browser can't run reG2

    A recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari has everything it needs.

    reG2 is open in another tab

    Only one tab can use the game files at a time, so that saves don't overwrite each other.

    Your copy of GTA2

    Drop it here, or choose it:

    A GTA2 folder, GTA2INSTALLER.exe (the free 2004 release), the original CD or its .iso, or a .zip or .7z of an installed game.

    I don't have the game

    Rockstar gave GTA2 away for free in 2004. The Internet Archive keeps that release: download GTA2INSTALLER.exe (about 350 MB) from the page below, then choose it here. You don't need to run it.

    archive.org/details/gta2_rockstar-classics

    Nothing is uploaded: the files stay in your browser.
